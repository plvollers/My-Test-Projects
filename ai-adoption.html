How to read this

  • What this measures: visible company AI activity in Slack’s #cipher_ai, not Cursor token spend. Local Cursor/Claude usage that never shows up in Slack is invisible here.
  • Why this source: CipherHealth does not expose Cursor admin analytics through current connectors. #cipher_ai is the company AI channel Kate Huber launched 5 Jun 2026; the Cursor enterprise trial went live 26 Jun.
  • Department mapping: Salesforce UserRole and title (CSM → Customer Success, Implementation → Professional Services, CRE/SDR → Sales, Technology/Engineering → Technology, etc.).
  • August dip: matches the 19 Aug spend guardrails ($50 non-eng / $200 eng, Grok + Composer only). Several people hit monthly limits (Emma Chowns 17 Aug; CS leadership asking what to do after the cap).
  • Technology stays high: Kate (AI ops), engineering, and platform folks keep posting product/connector updates, so Tech is both usage and enablement traffic.
  • Customer Success: strong July (trial + wins: transition docs, customer one-pagers) then fewer unique posters in Aug/Sep even as CS leadership is running dedicated AI enablement.
  • Cursor admin spend by user/department would be the gold-standard usage chart. Kate or a Cursor org admin can export that from the Cursor dashboard to overlay on this.
